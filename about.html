Kalpesh Katare/About

01

Beyond thematerial.

I'm an emerging polymer engineer studying how long-chain molecules become the bottles, films, fibres and parts that shape everyday life — and how they could be made smarter and more sustainable.

I study B.Tech in Plastic & Polymer Engineering at the Maharashtra Institute of Technology. My interest sits where chemistry meets manufacturing: how structure at the molecular scale decides the properties of a finished part, and how processing can make or break a material.

I'm drawn toward research — reading the literature closely, comparing methods, and spotting the questions that haven't been answered yet. Alongside engineering, I bring hands-on experience in printing and a designer's eye for graphics and visual communication.

People whomoved me.

Two builders whose stories changed how I think. Tap a card to flip it.

Co-founder, Apple

Steve Jobs

Started Apple in a garage in 1976, was pushed out of his own company in 1985, built NeXT and Pixar, then came back in 1997 to lead the iMac, iPod and iPhone.

1976Apple1985NeXT1997Return2007iPhone
What I took from it
How Steve shaped me

Design is how it works.

He showed me that engineering and design are the same job. A product should feel simple even when the inside is complex — that is how I try to build JobFlow. And getting knocked down is not the end of the story; it can be where the next idea starts.

Co-founder, OnePlus · Founder, Nothing

Carl Pei

Co-founded OnePlus in 2013 while still in his early twenties and grew it by building a loyal community first. In 2021 he started again from scratch with Nothing, taking on the giants with transparent, playful design.

2013OnePlus2020Moves on2021Nothing2022Phone (1)
What I took from it
How Carl shaped me

Start young. Build the community first.

He taught me that I do not need to wait until I am older or have everything figured out. Bring people together early and the product grows with them — that is the idea behind Polycomm. Being different on purpose is a strategy, not a risk.
